- **Grader calibration.** The "judge is an unevaluated classifier" section replaces "illustratively 50 rows" with the following rules:
  - Use at least 60 rows per failure mode, and about 100 when possible.
  - Every failure mode needs rows of both classes.
  - Score the grader as TPR and TNR separately, never as raw agreement. A BAD/GOOD worked example shows why.
  - Recalibrate when the grader model, the grader's prompt or the model under test changes.

- **Trajectory assertions.** New subsection "Assert the path, not only the answer". It covers `Result<T>.toolExecutions()` and the `ToolExecution` accessors `request().name()`, `request().arguments()`, `result()` and `hasFailed()`.
