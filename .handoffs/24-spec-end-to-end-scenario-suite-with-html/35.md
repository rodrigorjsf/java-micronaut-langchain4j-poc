## What was built
The `tool-search-rollout` skill no longer tells readers to set a semantic `minScore` floor through
`VectorToolSearchStrategy.Builder`. It now states that the builder has no such setter (floor fixed at
`0.0`, which filters nothing because relevance is `(cosine + 1) / 2`), explains why subclassing
(`search()` reads a private field; only `format()` is protected) and wrapping (`ToolSearchResult`
carries no scores) do not help, and teaches a custom `ToolSearchStrategy` with a short sketch. A
terminology note defines `minScore` as "the score floor, wherever it lives", and the gate, query-set
and failure-signature rows point at the custom strategy under semantic search.
